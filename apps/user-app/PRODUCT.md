# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Everyday consumers looking for a fast, reliable way to split bills, pay friends, or manage digital funds. Users who value modern aesthetics (like Google Pay or Apple Cash) over clunky traditional banking apps.

## Product Purpose
A highly secure, modern digital wallet allowing users to add/withdraw funds and seamlessly transfer money to peers through an intuitive chat-based interface.

## Brand Personality
Modern, Premium, Dynamic, Trustworthy, Fast.

## Anti-references
Clunky traditional banking apps. Generic AI slop (purple gradients, bento grids without purpose). Blocky forms that navigate away from context.

## Design Principles
- Context Retention: Users should not leave their current view for transient actions (use Modals).
- Conversational Finance: Transactions are inherently social; P2P payments belong in a chat flow.
- Premium Feel: Smooth animations, high contrast, clean typography.
- Frictionless Confidence: Clear error states, instant optimistic UI, bold visual feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast minimums for all text and form fields. Reduced motion fallbacks for all animations. Clear error states inline.
